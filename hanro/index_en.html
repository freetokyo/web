Hanro
See your daily earnings instantly

The all-in-one earnings tracker for gig delivery drivers. Multi-platform support, screenshot earnings import, take-home simulation, and tax-ready data export — all running fully on-device.

View on App Store

Key Features

Multi-platform Support

Track earnings across Uber Eats, Wolt, menu, Amazon Flex, and any custom service in one place.

Screenshot Earnings Import

Share an earnings screenshot from your delivery app and Hanro reads the amount and logs it — no retyping.

Mileage Tracking

Core Motion + Core Location measure distance from work start to end. Swipe to classify each session as business or personal. Automatic drive detection can be switched on in Settings (Premium).

Take-home Simulation

Estimates income tax, resident tax, national health insurance and pension from this year's records, then shows your gross and take-home hourly rate side by side.

Peak-time Insights

Estimates your hourly rate for the current weekday and time slot from your own past records, plus an earnings map of where you did well (Premium).

Tax-ready Data Export

CSV formats for Japanese tax software (freee, MoneyForward, Yayoi). Pick the target year so previous years never leak in. Automatic household-allocation.

Receipt OCR

Scan a receipt to read the amount and store and pre-select the expense category (Premium). Monthly PDF reports per expense category.

Statutory Driving Records

Daily driving report PDF in Japan's MLIT format, plus accident / near-miss report PDFs with a quick check on whether filing is required.

Vehicle Management

Local reminders for inspection and oil-change due dates. Fuel economy and average fuel price computed from your refuelling log.

Fully Offline

All processing on-device. Nothing is sent to our servers. If you are signed in to iCloud, your data syncs across your devices.

Apple Watch

Log deliveries with one tap from your wrist. Large buttons designed for use while driving.

Widgets, Live Activities & Siri

Today's earnings and monthly goal on Home Screen. Active sessions on the Lock Screen and in Control Center. Start a shift by asking Siri.
