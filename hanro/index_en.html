Key Features

Multi-platform Support

Track earnings across Uber Eats, Wolt, menu, Amazon Flex, and any custom service in one place.

Auto Mileage Tracking

Core Motion + Core Location + vehicle Bluetooth automatically detects driving and splits business/personal km.

AI Peak-time Prediction

Apple Intelligence analyzes day-of-week × time-of-day × weather to suggest high-yield slots.

Tax-ready Data Export

CSV formats for Japanese tax software (freee, MoneyForward, Yayoi). Automatic household-allocation.

Receipt OCR

Scan receipts → VisionKit + Foundation Models auto-categorize. Monthly PDF reports per expense category.

Fully Offline

All processing on-device. No data leaves your device. Optional iCloud Private DB sync.

Apple Watch

Log deliveries with one tap from your wrist. Large buttons designed for use while driving.

Widgets & Live Activities

Today's earnings and monthly goal on Home Screen. Active sessions on Lock Screen.
