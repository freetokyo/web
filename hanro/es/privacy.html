Política de privacidad

Hanro - 配達ドライバーの稼ぎ管理

Última actualización: 2026 / Desarrollador: CHEN YANGLI (freetokyo)

1. Basic Policy

Hanro is designed with user privacy as the top priority. All data is processed only on your device. We do not send data to our servers, disclose information to third parties, or use any advertising or analytics SDKs.

2. Information We Access

The following information is accessed on-device to provide app features.

None of this leaves your device.

Location: to automatically record work mileage for expense calculation, including background location during a work session.

Motion and Fitness: to detect car travel and automatically estimate work start and end.

Bluetooth: to detect connection with registered in-vehicle devices and estimate work start and end.

Camera: to photograph receipts for automatic expense entry; images are processed on-device only.

Notifications: to send reminders for vehicle inspection, oil changes, etc.

User-entered data: delivery records, sales, expenses, vehicle information, goals, tax settings, etc.

, stored in a local database on your device.

3. iCloud Sync

Only when you enable iCloud Sync: your data is synced solely within your own iCloud account via Apple's CloudKit Private Database. The developer cannot access this data.

4. Apple Intelligence and Foundation Models

Peak-time prediction and receipt OCR classification use Apple's on-device AI (Foundation Models). All processing is completed on your device and nothing is sent to external servers.

5. Third-Party Disclosure

We do not disclose user information to third parties except as required by law.

6. Tracking

Hanro does not engage in tracking under App Tracking Transparency. We do not use advertising identifiers (IDFA), analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking.

7. Children's Privacy

The primary users of this app are adult business drivers. We do not intentionally collect information from children under 13.

8. Data Deletion

You can delete all on-device data by uninstalling the app from iOS Settings → General → iPhone Storage. iCloud-synced data can be deleted via iCloud settings in the app's iCloud data section.

9. Billing and App Store

Subscription billing is handled through Apple's App Store. We do not obtain payment information such as credit card details. These are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be revised as needed. Important changes will be notified in-app or on the support page.
