Hanro - 配達ドライバーの稼ぎ管理

Offline is használható?
Igen. Az összes fő funkció offline is működik. Az adatok csak az eszközön tárolódnak, és nem kerülnek a fejlesztő szervereire.
Can I sync data across iPhone and iPad?
Yes. Enable Settings → iCloud Sync and data will automatically sync across devices signed in to the same Apple ID. Synced data is managed only within your own Apple CloudKit Private Database.
How accurate is automatic mileage recording?
The app combines Core Motion (accelerometer), Core Location (GPS), and Bluetooth (vehicle connection detection) to detect driving. Accuracy varies by device condition, signal strength, and driving environment. Manual correction is also available.
Which devices support AI peak-time prediction?
Devices compatible with Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro / 16 Pro / 17 Pro and later). On incompatible devices, a statistics-based fallback is used.
Which accounting software supports the CSV export for tax filing?
The CSV output is compatible with freee, MoneyForward, and Yayoi Accounting import formats. Import the exported CSV into your accounting software.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Go to iPhone Settings → tap your account name → Subscriptions → select Hanro → tap Cancel Subscription. No charge if cancelled during the free trial period.
How do I restore my purchase on another device?
Sign in with the same Apple ID, then go to App Settings → Restore Purchase.
Can I back up my data?
When iCloud Sync is enabled, data is automatically backed up to iCloud. CSV and PDF export is also available via Records → Data Export.
How do I connect Hanro with Apple Watch?
Launch Hanro on your iPhone and approve the Hanro installation in the Watch app. One-tap recording from your Apple Watch is then available.
Is there a corporate plan or multi-driver management?
The current version is designed for sole traders and freelancers. Corporate and multi-driver management is planned for future updates. Please send feature requests by email.
