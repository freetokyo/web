Privacy Policy

Hanro - 配達ドライバーの稼ぎ管理

Last updated: May 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy ("this Policy") sets out how CHEN YANGLI ("we") handles user information within the mobile application "Hanro" ("this app").

1. Basic Policy

This app is designed with user privacy as the top priority, and in principle all data is processed only on the user's device. We do not transmit data to our servers, provide information to third parties, or use advertising or analytics SDKs.

2. Information We Collect

This app collects and uses the following information on-device to provide its features.

This information never leaves the device.

• Location: Automatically records driving distance during work hours and uses it for expense calculation.

Background location is also used to record driving distance from work start to end.

• Motion & Fitness: Detects vehicle movement to automatically estimate work start/end.

• Bluetooth: Detects connections to registered in-vehicle devices to automatically estimate work start/end.

• Camera: Photographs receipts for automatic expense entry.

Captured images are processed on-device only.

• Notifications: Used to send reminders such as vehicle inspection deadlines and oil change timing.

• User-entered data: Delivery records, revenue, expenses, vehicle information, goals, tax settings, and other information entered directly by the user is stored in an on-device database.

3. iCloud Sync

Only if the user enables iCloud Sync, the above data is synced solely within the user's own iCloud account via Apple's CloudKit Private Database. We never have access to this data.

4. Apple Intelligence / Foundation Models

Earning-time prediction and receipt OCR classification use Apple's on-device AI (Foundation Models). All of this processing completes entirely on the user's device and is never sent to external servers.

5. Disclosure to Third Parties

We do not disclose user information to third parties except where required by law.

6. Tracking

This app does not track under App Tracking Transparency. We use no advertising identifiers (IDFA), analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking whatsoever.

7. Children's Information

This app is primarily intended for adult drivers who are business operators. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

8. Data Deletion

Users can delete all on-device data by uninstalling this app via iOS Settings → General → iPhone Storage. Synced data in iCloud can be erased by deleting the app's data from iCloud settings.

9. Billing and the App Store

Subscription billing for this app is handled through Apple's App Store, and we never obtain payment information such as credit card details. This information is handled in accordance with Apple's privacy policy.

10. Changes to this Policy

The content of this Policy may change as needed. Material changes will be announced within the app or on the support page.
