Hanro - Gig Driver Tracker

Gig driver income & tax export

Download on the App Store

Hanro is the earnings management app built for gig delivery drivers — food delivery, courier, ride-share, and taxi. Designed for Japan's tax system, it works equally well for any driver who needs clean records.

Log earnings across multiple platforms with one tap. Hanro automatically computes your hourly rate and per-km rate from active hours and distance. AI peak-time predictions and one-tap tax export help you maximize income and minimize tax-season stress.

KEY FEATURES

Multi-Platform Earnings Log

  • Compatible with Uber Eats and all major delivery and courier platforms; add any custom service by name
  • Per-platform breakdown of earnings, trip count, and hourly rate

Automatic Mileage Tracking

  • Core Motion + Core Location detect driving automatically — no manual start needed
  • Bluetooth vehicle pairing marks the start and end of each work session
  • Business and personal mileage split automatically for tax deductions

AI Peak-Time Prediction

  • Apple Intelligence analyzes day-of-week, time-of-day, and weather patterns
  • Monthly review surfaces your highest-yield slots and proposes next month's schedule

Tax-Ready CSV Export

  • CSV formats compatible with freee, MoneyForward, and Yayoi (Japanese tax software)
  • Automatic business-use ratio calculator (business km / total km)
  • Built with Japan's qualified invoice system and e-bookkeeping law in mind

Expense Management

  • Scan receipts → AI auto-categorization (VisionKit + Foundation Models)
  • Track vehicle, fuel, telecom, and other deductible expense categories
  • Export monthly PDF reports

Fully Offline

  • Every feature runs entirely on-device
  • No data leaves your iPhone
  • Optional iCloud sync across your devices

Apple Watch

  • Log a delivery with one tap from your wrist
  • Large buttons designed for safe use while driving

Widgets & Live Activities

  • Today's earnings and monthly goal progress on your Home Screen
  • Active work sessions displayed on the Lock Screen
