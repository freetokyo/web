What is Khaylog

Bring together the records for your horses that tend to scatter across paper diaries and spreadsheets — all in one app. Khaylog reminds you of farrier and vaccination due dates, and groups costs by currency (no exchange conversion is applied). You can record everything from an Arabian horse's 5-generation pedigree to show results and breeding. All data is stored on your device, and no account is required.

Key features

Horse profiles

Breed, sex, date of birth, colour, microchip/passport number and photo. 2 horses free, unlimited with Pro.

Farrier & health reminders

Local reminders ahead of the farrier cycle (4–8 weeks) and vaccination, deworming and insurance-renewal due dates.

Cost ledger (by currency)

Category roll-ups by month and year for livery, feed, vet, insurance, shows and more. JPY/USD/GBP/EUR/SAR/AED.

5-generation pedigree chart

Manual-entry pedigree tree with PDF export. In Arabic the lineage mirrors right-to-left.

Show & breeding records

Event, class, placing and judge. Covering, conception and expected foaling date (about 340 days as a guide).

Export & migration

Health-record PDF (to share with your vet), cost CSV, and JSON export/import of your data.

Privacy

Khaylog is fully offline. All records are stored solely on your device — there is no transmission to external servers and no data collection whatsoever.

Please note

This app is a record-keeping tool. Vaccination and deworming cycles, expected foaling dates and the like are general guides only; please consult a veterinarian for decisions about diagnosis, medication and breeding. Cost summaries are general information, not tax advice.
