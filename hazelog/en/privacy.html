Privacy Policy

HazeLog (the "app") is designed with privacy first. The app collects no personal information and no usage data whatsoever.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect or transmit any personal information, including your name, email address, contacts, location or device identifiers. No account is required.

Your roast records

Everything you enter — green beans, roast batches, temperatures, events and tasting notes — is stored only on your device. Neither the developer nor any third party can access it. The app makes no network requests and works fully in airplane mode.

Analytics and advertising

No analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no crash-reporting SDK are used. There is no tracking.

Sharing with third parties

Since no data is collected, nothing is shared or sold.

Exporting and deleting data

You can export your records as CSV or PDF yourself; the destination is chosen by you in the system share sheet, and the app never uploads anything. Deleting the app deletes the records stored on your device.

Purchases

The HazeLog Pro purchase is handled entirely by Apple's App Store. The app never receives your payment details. See Apple's privacy policy for how purchases are handled.

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone.
