Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Dartrack ("the app") is built to keep your household records private. We do not operate any server that receives your data.

No data collection

The app does not collect personal information or usage data. Everything you enter — staff names, roles, dates, work hours, leave, notes and pay figures — is stored only on your device. There is no account and no sign-in.

On-device storage and backup

Your records are saved locally and are included in your device's standard iCloud backup, which is managed by Apple, not by us. You can export your records to CSV or PDF at any time to keep your own copy.

Subscriptions

The optional Dartrack Pro subscription is processed by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit). We do not receive your payment details, and no records you enter in the app are transmitted for billing or any other purpose. Purchase and subscription status are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

No third-party SDKs

The app contains no analytics SDKs and no advertising SDKs.

Children

The app is a record-keeping tool for adults and is not directed at children.
