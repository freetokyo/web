Frequently asked questions

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything stays on your device. No account, no server sync, no data collection. Your records are included in your iPhone's iCloud backup and can be exported to CSV or PDF anytime.

Does Dartrack file visas or pay salaries?

No. Dartrack is a private record-keeping tool. It does not file visas or contracts, process wage payments (WPS), or provide legal advice. Labor values shown are adjustable references only — confirm current rules at mohre.gov.ae or with a legal professional.

What is Dartrack Pro?

An optional monthly or yearly subscription that unlocks unlimited staff, deadline reminders, payroll and end-of-service estimates, evaluation notes and export. The free plan includes one staff member, and you keep viewing and exporting your data even after canceling.
