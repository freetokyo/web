Dartrack

A quiet, private ledger for your household staff.

Dartrack helps households in the UAE that employ domestic staff — maids, drivers, nannies, cooks, gardeners and more — keep clear, private records. Everything stays on your own device: no account, no server sync, no data collection.

What you can do
  • Keep staff profiles: name, role and employment start date
  • Track visa and contract deadlines, with reminders 60, 30 and 7 days ahead (Pro)
  • Log work hours with a simple daily-rest and weekly-rest check
  • Estimate pay from an hourly or monthly rate (Pro)
  • Record annual and sick leave, with remaining days at a glance
  • Keep dated evaluation notes (Pro) and export any record to CSV or PDF (Pro)
Private by design

Your records are stored only on your iPhone and are included in your device's iCloud backup. The only network use is Apple's StoreKit for the optional subscription — no user records are ever transmitted.

Dartrack Pro

The free plan includes one staff member and core records. Dartrack Pro (monthly or yearly subscription) unlocks unlimited staff, deadline reminders, payroll estimates, evaluation notes and export. You can keep viewing and exporting your data even after canceling.

Dartrack keeps private records. It does not file visas or contracts, process wage payments (WPS), or provide legal advice. Labor-standard values shown are adjustable references only — confirm current rules at mohre.gov.ae or with a legal professional.
