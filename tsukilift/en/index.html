Move with your moon.

Just as the moon moves the tides, your own monthly rhythm moves your body. Understanding that natural cycle and putting it to work in the gym — that is the idea behind TsukiLift. With records and trends that follow your rhythm, get the most out of the person you are today.

Five kinds of records

Key features that link your cycle and your training

🌸
Cycle

Cycle tracking

Log start and end dates, flow, and pain in a few taps. Your past records show you the shape of your cycle.

💪
Training

Workout logging

Record exercise, weight, reps, and RPE set by set. Estimated 1RM and workout templates keep sessions efficient.

🌡️
BBT

Basal body temperature

Log your temperature each morning and see how it shifts across the cycle on a clear graph.

📊
Body

Body composition & condition

Track weight, body fat, and skeletal muscle mass, along with mood, energy, and sleep.

❤️
HealthKit

HealthKit integration

Connect to Apple Health to bring your existing health data into TsukiLift (entirely optional).

✦
Premium

Advanced analysis

Performance trends by cycle phase and deeper insights to fine-tune your training.

Who it's for

🌸

If your condition shifts with your cycle

Record and review how you feel in each phase, and get to know your own rhythm.

💪

Manage weight, reps, and RPE set by set. Estimated 1RM makes your progress visible.
