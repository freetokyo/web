Koegaki - Meeting Minutes

On-device AI transcription

Descarcă din App Store

Koegaki records your meetings and automatically generates transcripts, key points, decisions, and action items—all on your iPhone or iPad. No cloud, no server, no account needed.

Features

  • In-app recording (continues with screen off)
  • On-device transcription (with timestamps; tap to jump to that moment)
  • 3-section AI summary: Key Points / Decisions / To-Dos
  • Import existing audio files
  • Edit transcripts, summaries, and titles
  • Full-text search
  • Export as Text, Markdown, or PDF
  • Audio file management

Privacy First

Everything is processed on your device. No data collection, no external transmission. Ideal for confidential meetings.

One-Time Purchase (No Subscription)

60 minutes free per month. Buy once to unlock unlimited recording, transcription, and summarization—no recurring fees.

Note

AI summarization requires an Apple Intelligence-compatible device (iPhone 15 Pro/Max, iPhone 16+, iPad mini A17 Pro, iPad M1+). Transcription and other features work on all supported devices.
