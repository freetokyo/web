ユラログ / Yuralog

Menopause symptom log & doctor-visit notes

Turn your changing days into a record that is yours alone.

A personal record-keeping app that logs and visualizes every kind of menopause symptom without a single byte leaving your device — ready to show your doctor as-is at your visit. It does not judge. It records.

Just record

Log hot flashes and sweating / mood and anxiety / body and joints / sleep by intensity 0–3, every day. Notes and tags too. Logging is unlimited and free.

Ready for your visit

Turn your trends into a PDF report to show your doctor. Hand over the story a few minutes of appointment can't capture.

Fully local

All records stay on your device only. No account, no cloud, no ads, no data collection.

One-time purchase

Not a subscription. Buy once, use forever.
