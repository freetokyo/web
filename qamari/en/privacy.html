Qamari

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Qamari (the "app") works entirely offline and collects no data whatsoever. This policy states that in writing.

1. What we collect

Nothing. The app collects no personal information, no usage analytics and no device identifiers. There is no account to create — there is no sign-in at all. No analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no crash-reporting SDK is included.

2. Where your data lives

Your events, occasion settings and sighting adjustments are stored only on your device. There is no developer server, and nowhere for the data to be sent. Every feature works in airplane mode.

3. Device permissions

  • Notifications: used to deliver your reminders. They are local notifications; their contents never leave your device. If you decline, everything except notifications still works.
  • Calendar: used only to add events to your calendar, and only when you use the export feature. The app does not read your existing events and does not send anything anywhere. This permission is optional.
  • Typing a date naturally: what you type is processed on your device to fill in the date field. It is never transmitted.

4. Sharing with third parties

Because no data is collected, none is shared, sold or disclosed. The app does not track you and shows no ads.

5. Purchases

Pro is purchased through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment; the developer never receives your card details. The app only stores whether the purchase is active, based on your device and your Apple purchase history.

6. Children

Because the app collects no data, it is safe to use at any age.

7. Changes to this policy

Any change to this policy will be announced on this page.
