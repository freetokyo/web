1. What this app is for

Qamari (the "app") is a tool for managing dates, showing the Hijri (Islamic) calendar alongside the Gregorian one.

The app does not provide religious advice, interpretation or rulings. Dates and names of occasions vary by region and by school of thought, and the app does not present any of them as the correct one. The dates shown are for reference, based on the calculation method you select (Umm al-Qura, Civil or Tabular) and on the sighting adjustment you set.

A calculated calendar and the moon sighting announced in your area can differ. Final decisions about fasting, prayer, occasions and any other religious practice rest with you and with the school of thought and local authorities you follow. Please do not rely on this app's display alone.
