Getting started

Use the + on the Assets tab to add cash, deposits, stocks, real estate and more. On the Trend tab, record this month’s snapshot to see your net-worth chart. The Inventory & Estate tab offers the property-inventory PDF and the inheritance-tax estimate (Full Access).

FAQ

Is it free to use?
Yes. The asset ledger and net-worth trend are free. Premium asset classes, the inventory PDF, the inheritance-tax estimate and CSV export are unlocked with a one-time Full Access purchase (¥3,000).

How do I restore a purchase?
Go to Settings → Restore Purchase. Full Access is non-consumable, so it restores at no extra cost with the same Apple ID.

Where is my data stored?
Everything is stored on your device, with no external transmission or data collection. The app works fully in Airplane Mode.

How accurate is the tax estimate?
It is a personal simulation of the total inheritance tax. It does not reflect the spousal credit or special provisions and differs from an actual filing. Consult a tax accountant for official decisions.
