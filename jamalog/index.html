Jamalog

Every camel, on the record. A private, fully-offline logbook — no account, no data collection.

What you can do

Profiles

Name, breed, sex, birth date, RFID/chip and photo for every camel.

Health records

Vaccinations, medication, injuries, check-ups, temperature and weight — records only, not veterinary advice.

Bloodline

Build a pedigree chart from each camel's sire and dam, and see offspring.

Races

Log rank, time and prize, with wins, podiums and win rate.

Reminders

Vaccinations, check-ups, races and breeding — local notifications, no server.

Documents

Keep registration, insurance and photos on your device.

Private by design

Everything you enter is stored only on your iPhone. There is no account, no server sync and no data collection. Jamalog is a private logbook — it is not an official or government registry and does not replace one.
