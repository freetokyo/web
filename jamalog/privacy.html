Privacy Policy

Jamalog · Last updated: July 2026

Jamalog (the “App”) is designed to be private by default. Your privacy is the point of the product.

Data we collect

None. The App does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. There is no account and no sign-in. Everything you enter — camel profiles, health records, pedigree, race records, documents, photos and reminders — is stored only on your device.

Photos

If you add a photo, it is read from your photo library only with your permission and stored locally in the App. Photos are never uploaded or shared by the App.

Analytics & tracking

The App contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs and no third-party trackers. We do not build a profile of you and we do not track you across apps or websites.

Notifications

Reminders are delivered as local notifications scheduled on your device. No reminder content leaves your device.

Subscriptions

Jamalog Pro is an auto-renewable subscription sold through Apple. Payment and billing are handled entirely by Apple under Apple’s terms; the App does not receive your payment details. See Apple’s Privacy Policy for how Apple processes purchases.

Children

The App is a general-purpose record-keeping tool and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Not an official registry

Jamalog is a private, personal logbook. It is not an official or government registry and does not replace one.

Contact

Questions? Please use the support page.
