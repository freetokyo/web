Privacy Policy

2026年7月 / July 2026

The developer collects no data

Inkarte collects no personal information or usage data about you or your clients. It uses no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs and no tracking. No account is required.

Everything stays on your device

All data — client records, session history, consent forms and photos — is stored only on your iPhone/iPad. Nothing is sent to any external server. If you use backup export, the destination (e.g. the Files app) is under your control.

You are the data controller

Inkarte is a business tool for you, the artist, to record client information. Responsibility for handling, managing and protecting your clients' health and personal information rests with you as the data controller. Compliance with applicable data-protection law is your responsibility.
