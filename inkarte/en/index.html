From consult to consent — your whole studio, in one app.

Get it on the App Store

Features

Client records

Contact, social handle, health screening (allergies/medications/history/keloid), skin notes and tags. Alerts pinned to the top of the karte.

Appointments

One calendar for everything, with reminders and optional export to your device Calendar (initials only by default for privacy).

Session history

Body part, price, machine/needle, inks used (brand/color/lot) and photos (design, stencil, immediately after, healed, other).

Consent & signature

Check items from a standard or custom template, then capture a handwritten signature on screen and save as PDF. The signing screen is a guest mode only the artist can exit.

Ink lot search

Find every client a specific lot was used on — useful for follow-up.

Aftercare reminders

Follow-up notifications a chosen number of days after a session. General care reminders, not medical advice.

Statistics

Monthly revenue, session count and style breakdown, computed on device.

Bulk export & backup

Export all data as CSV or combine consent PDFs, plus an encrypted archive you can restore with your passphrase.

Pricing

The free tier supports up to 10 clients and 3 signed consent forms per calendar month, with unlimited sessions, appointments and backup/restore. A one-time Full Access purchase makes clients and consent forms unlimited and unlocks statistics, custom templates, portfolio and bulk export. There is no subscription.

All data is stored on your device with no collection or transmission by the developer. You, the artist, are the data controller for your clients' information.

The consent templates are general samples; they do not guarantee any specific legal effect and are not legal advice. For actual use, please consult a professional according to your local regulations.
