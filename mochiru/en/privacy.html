📌 Basic Policy

This policy explains how information is handled in 'Mochiru Asset Lifetime Simulator' (hereinafter 'the App').

📂 Information the App Stores

The App stores the following, all only on your device.

• Simulation assumptions you entered (asset amounts, savings, withdrawal settings, investment assumptions, inflation rate, etc.

) • Scenarios and results you created and saved • Purchase status (whether premium has been purchased).

This information is never sent to the developer's servers.

🚫 Information Not Collected

This app collects none of the following.

• Personal identification information such as name, email, or phone number • Financial account or login information (no account linking) • Location or GPS data • Contacts or calendar • Behavioral logs or analytics data • Advertising identifier (IDFA).

Figures you enter are used only for simulation on your device.

📡 Network Communication

This app works entirely offline. No external network communication occurs for any function.

💳 Purchases and Payments

In-app purchases (one-time premium) are processed through Apple's App Store. Payment information is managed by Apple and is not provided to the developer. StoreKit 2 is used to verify and restore purchases.

🗑️ Data Storage and Deletion

Data is stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all stored data. Please back up important data yourself.

🔗 Provision to Third Parties

Since this app collects no personal information, it has no information to provide to third parties.

📝 Policy Changes

If this policy is revised, we will notify you via an app update or on this page.

For privacy questions, please contact us via the support page.
