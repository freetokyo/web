Политика конфиденциальности

RecoverySync 回復スコア

Последнее обновление: 2026 г. / Разработчик: CHEN YANGLI (freetokyo)

What data we access

With your permission, RecoverySync reads the following data from Apple HealthKit on your device: Heart Rate Variability (RMSSD) — to calculate your recovery score; Resting Heart Rate — to calculate your recovery score; Sleep data — to calculate your recovery score.

All access is read-only.

RecoverySync never writes data back to HealthKit.

What we do NOT collect

RecoverySync does not collect any of the following: names, email addresses, or account information; location data; IP addresses or device identifiers; analytics or usage data; crash reports sent to third-party services; advertising identifiers.

Where your data is stored

All data generated by RecoverySync — including your recovery scores and preferences — is stored exclusively on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework.

No data is ever transmitted to external servers.

iCloud Backup of your device may include this data at your discretion under your own Apple ID and Apple's privacy terms.

Third-party SDKs and services

RecoverySync contains no third-party analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or tracking libraries. The only external framework used is Apple's HealthKit, which is governed by Apple's own privacy policies.

Apple HealthKit

Health data obtained through HealthKit is used solely for the purpose of calculating and displaying your recovery score within the app. This data is never used for advertising, shared with data brokers, or disclosed to third parties.

Children

RecoverySync is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this privacy policy, we will update the Last updated date above. Continued use of the app after such changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
