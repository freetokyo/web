Built for GCC households

Two calendars, side by side

Your iqama expires on a Hijri (Umm al-Qura) date, your passport on a Gregorian one. Muddah shows both together, so a conversion mistake never costs you a renewal.

Presets for local documents

Iqama, visa, Emirates ID, istimara, Fahes, driving license, passport and health, car & home insurance — ready to add in a tap.

Your whole family

Track documents for yourself, your spouse, children and household workers — all sorted by what expires next, in one dashboard.

Reminders before it's late

Multi-stage on-device reminders as each expiry approaches. Names can be hidden from lock-screen notifications for privacy.

Fully offline & private

Everything stays on your device. No account, no sync, no data collection. Protect the app with Face ID.

Renewal checklists

Some renewals need prerequisites first — Fahes and insurance before istimara. Muddah flags them so nothing surprises you at the window.
