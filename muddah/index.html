Built for GCC households

Two calendars, side by side

Your iqama expires on a Hijri (Umm al-Qura) date, your passport on a Gregorian one. Muddah shows both together and converts between them, so a reading mistake never costs you a renewal.

One tap when you renew

Renewed something? Tap once and Muddah works out the new expiry on the calendar that document actually uses. A Hijri year is about 354 days, not 365, so you do not have to count it yourself. Every renewal is kept as history.

Presets for local documents

Iqama, visa, Emirates ID, istimara, Fahes, driving license, passport and health, car or home insurance — ready to add in a tap.

Your whole family

Track documents for yourself, your spouse, children and household workers — all sorted by what expires next. Search, filter by status, and archive what you no longer need.

Reminders and a widget

Multi-stage on-device reminders warn you before each expiry, at the time of day you choose. Names can be hidden from lock-screen notifications. A Home Screen widget shows what expires next without opening the app.

Renewal checklists

Some renewals need things in order first — Fahes, insurance and traffic fines before istimara. Muddah keeps them as a personal checklist so nothing surprises you at the window.

Scan and read the date

Scan a document to a multi-page PDF and let Muddah read the expiry date off it, in either calendar, for you to confirm. It all happens on your device.

Fully offline and private

Everything stays on your device. No account, no sync, no data collection. Protect the app with Face ID.
