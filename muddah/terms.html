What Muddah is

Muddah is a personal tool for recording your own documents and their expiry dates and for reminding you before they expire. It is not affiliated with any government or government service, and it does not provide legal, immigration, or any other professional advice. Always confirm official requirements, dates, and procedures with the relevant authority.

The renewal checklists in the app are a personal aid, not a statement of official requirements. Requirements vary by country, year and sponsor.

Reminders

Reminders are delivered on your device by iOS and are subject to iOS limitations, including a cap on how many can be pending at once. Muddah schedules the nearest ones and refreshes them whenever you open the app, but does not guarantee that every reminder will be delivered. You remain responsible for renewing your documents on time.

Reading dates from a scan

When you scan a document, Muddah may suggest expiry dates it recognised in the image. These are suggestions only and are never applied automatically. Please check any suggested date against the document itself before saving it.
