Information we collect

This app collects no personal information or usage data. There is no network permission, no external transmission, and no account required.

Photo library access

The app accesses the photo library only within the scope you select, for the purpose of loading and organizing screenshots and optionally deleting originals to free up storage. Access is limited to on-device processing; images are never transmitted externally.

Information stored on-device

Extracted results (titles, summaries, categories, recognized text, etc.) and thumbnails are stored only on your device. The original image remains in your photo library; the app holds only a reference to it.

Sensitive information

Content that may contain passwords or authentication codes is masked and not saved, summarized, or exported.

Third-party services, analytics, and advertising

Because no data is collected, none is shared with third parties. No analytics SDKs or advertising SDKs are used.
