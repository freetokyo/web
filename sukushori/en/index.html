Sukushori - Screenshot Organizer

Auto-sort your screenshots on device

Download on the App Store

Sukushori automatically organizes the screenshots that pile up unnoticed in your photo library — entirely on your device, with no data sent outside.

What it does

  • Reads screenshots from your photo library and auto-sorts them by content
  • Categories: places to visit · wishlists · events & dates · receipts & amounts · recipes · memos & ideas
  • Extracts key text: shop names, addresses, URLs, prices, dates, and more
  • One-tap actions from the detail view: open in Maps, open URL, add to Calendar/Reminders, copy
  • Full-text search and category filter — all on device
  • Delete originals in bulk after organizing to reclaim storage (confirmation required)
  • Manual reclassification supported

Privacy first

  • Zero network communication (no network permission requested)
  • Images and extracted data are stored on your device only — never transmitted
  • Passwords and authentication codes are detected and hidden, never stored

About AI classification

  • Classification and summarization are assisted by on-device AI — text recognized in the screenshot only, no guessing or fabrication
  • AI auto-classification is available on Apple Intelligence-compatible devices (iOS 26 or later). On other devices, OCR and manual sorting are still available

Pricing

  • One-time purchase, no subscription. Try auto-classification for free; purchase to remove the limit and unlock bulk cleanup
  • No ads · No account required · Works offline

* This app assists in extracting recognized text. Accuracy is not guaranteed. Please verify important information yourself.
