Fortrolighedspolitik

HomeNet Navi - 世帯手取ナビ

Sidst opdateret: 2026 / Udvikler: CHEN YANGLI (freetokyo)

1. Introduction

The app calculates household take-home pay based on Japan's 2026 tax system.

All data is stored on-device only; no data is sent to external servers.

The app uses Apple StoreKit for subscriptions and UserNotifications for wall-approach alerts (on-device).

No advertising, no third-party tracking, no personal data collection.

2. Information We Handle

User-entered: household and spouse annual income; number and age category of dependents; various income deductions; municipality category (premium); saved scenario names and memos.

Auto-collected: first install date and launch count (on-device only); subscription status (via Apple StoreKit).

NOT collected: personal identifying information (name, address, phone, email); GPS location; contacts or address book; advertising identifier (IDFA); biometric data; browsing history or other app usage; cloud sync data (no server transmission).

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used solely for: calculating income tax, resident tax, and social insurance based on the 2026 system and displaying take-home amounts; household scenario comparison and saving; local notifications for wall approach (premium); subscription determination via StoreKit.

4. Where Data Is Stored

On your device only: input values and saved scenarios are stored in UserDefaults and FileManager (local storage on-device).

No server transmission: the app has no backend server and makes no Firebase or API calls.

iCloud / CloudKit: not used for family sharing or cross-device sync.

Whether iCloud backup includes app data follows Apple's device backup specifications.

5. Third-Party Services

The app uses Apple-provided services only: StoreKit / App Store (subscription billing); UserNotifications (wall-approach local notifications, on-device only). See apple.com/legal/privacy for Apple's privacy policy.

6. Data Retention

Data is retained on your device until the app is deleted. iCloud backup data follows Apple's data retention policy. All local data is deleted when you delete the app.

7. Your Rights

Data export: no export function is currently available (view and copy from the screen). Data deletion: deleting the app removes all local data. EU residents: GDPR rights apply. Japan residents: Personal Information Protection Act rights apply. Contact us below for assistance.

8. Children's Privacy

This app is for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

9. Handling of Calculation Results

Calculation results are simulations based on published system information. They are not guaranteed to match actual tax filing, year-end adjustment, or social insurance determination results. For important decisions, consult a tax accountant, social insurance labor consultant, or financial planner.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, please contact us via the support page.

11. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be revised as needed. Significant changes will be communicated via app update or this page.
