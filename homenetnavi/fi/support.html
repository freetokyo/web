HomeNet Navi - 世帯手取ナビ

Kuinka aloitan ilmaiseksi?
Lataa HomeNet Navi App Storesta ilman rekisteröintiä. Peruslaskelma, 5 seinää ja 1 skenaario ovat pysyvästi ilmaisia.
What are the minimum required inputs?
Just enter the household head's annual income and the spouse's annual income (salary) — only 2 fields — and the household take-home curve reflecting the income walls will appear. Dependents and additional deductions can be added later in Precise Input.
How much of the 2026 system is covered?
Major items of income tax, resident tax, and social insurance are calculated based on the 2026 system. Specifically: salary income deduction, basic deduction, spouse deduction, spouse special deduction (¥1.03M / ¥1.5M / ¥2.01M walls), health insurance and employee pension (¥1.06M / ¥1.3M walls), and resident tax deductions.
What are the 'walls'?
The app covers 5 walls: ¥1.03M — income tax threshold / full spouse deduction line; ¥1.06M — social insurance enrollment line for large-company part-timers; ¥1.3M — social insurance enrollment line when leaving dependents; ¥1.5M — full spouse special deduction line; ¥2.01M — upper limit of spouse special deduction. Each wall detail screen links directly to official NTA and Pension Bureau sources.
Is municipality variation in resident tax supported?
The default calculation uses the standard resident tax rate (10% income portion + flat rate). The premium plan also covers major municipality variations. For exact amounts, check official municipality information or your resident tax notification.
Can calculation results be used directly for tax filing?
This app is a simulator for working-style decision support. It cannot be used as official documents for tax filing or year-end adjustment. Always consult a tax accountant, social insurance labor consultant, or financial planner for important decisions.
How many scenarios can be saved?
Free plan: 1 scenario; premium plan: unlimited. Compare multiple working styles (e.g., 'current vs going full-time from part-time') and see the take-home difference.
What can be seen in the scenario comparison screen?
Each scenario's household take-home, plus the breakdown of income tax, resident tax, and social insurance, and the monetary difference between scenarios. Which walls were crossed or not crossed is also clearly shown in color.
What does 'Wall Approach Notification' notify?
With the premium plan, based on the year's cumulative salary, it sends a local notification like 'Only ¥XX more before crossing the wall.' All processing is on-device; nothing is sent to servers. Notifications can be toggled in Settings → Notifications.
Is there a free trial?
Premium monthly and annual plans include a 1-month free trial for the first month. You can cancel anytime during the trial and will retain premium access until the trial ends.
