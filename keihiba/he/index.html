Keihiba is a receipt organizer for freelancers and sole proprietors in Japan. Scan receipts, organize expenses, and export CSV or PDF reports — all offline, one-time purchase.

Scan + Organize

Capture receipts with your camera or import PDF/image files. On-device OCR reads date, amount, and merchant automatically.

Search + Summarize

Search by date, amount, or merchant. Monthly totals by expense category and tax rate.

Export

CSV (UTF-8 with BOM) and monthly PDF report.

100% Private

No server uploads, no data collection, no ads, no account. Works entirely offline.

Not legal or tax advice. Consult a certified accountant.

Pricing

Free for up to 10 receipts per month. One-time purchase for unlimited use.
