PrintSummary Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

PrintSummary helps you capture school handouts, extract text with OCR, organize deadlines, and manage submission status. This page explains what information the app handles, where it is stored, what is never transmitted externally, and what you can delete.

Core posture: Images and OCR text are not sent to external servers. Core processing stays on device.
Handled data: Images, OCR text, deadline candidates, submission status, review-related data, notification settings, and purchase status.
No external transmission: The app does not use proprietary cloud sync, ad SDKs, or third-party analytics SDKs.

How data is handled

1. Information the app handles

  • School handout images captured or imported by the user
  • Text extracted through OCR
  • Organizing data such as deadlines, items to bring, subjects, submission status, and review tags
  • On-device settings such as notifications, added vocabulary, recent history, and purchase status

2. Purpose of use

  • To read text from handout images and organize deadlines and items to bring
  • To search, revisit, and re-run OCR on saved handouts
  • To display local reminders before deadlines
  • To manage the free quota and Premium status on the device

3. Permissions

  • Camera: Used for capturing school handouts and running OCR on device.
  • Photos: Used for importing existing images and running OCR on device.
  • Notifications: Used for local reminders before deadlines.

Storage, sharing, and deletion

4. Storage location

Images are stored under the app's Application Support directory, OCR text and extraction candidates are stored in the on-device data store, and some settings are stored in UserDefaults. None of this leaves your device.

5. External transmission and sharing

Images used for OCR and the resulting OCR text are not sent to external APIs. Manual backup can export JSON, but only when you explicitly choose to share it.

6. Data deletion and contact

From Settings > Delete Data you can remove local data including saved handouts, OCR results, stored images, extraction issue samples, added vocabulary, and recent history.
