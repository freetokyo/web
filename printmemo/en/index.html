① Capture

Photograph school handouts on the spot or import them from your photo library. No account required.

② Organize with OCR

Text is extracted automatically to sort out deadlines, subjects, and items to bring. All processing stays on device — nothing is sent to external servers.

③ Never miss a deadline

Get local reminders before due dates. Saved handouts stay searchable so you can revisit them any time for review.

Core product message

  • Capture paper school handouts the moment they are received.
  • Use OCR to extract text and support deadline or submission review.
  • Search, revisit, and reuse saved handouts for study and follow-up.
  • Within the verified implementation scope, the core workflow is designed around on-device processing.
