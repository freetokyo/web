What it does

Measured against your personal baseline rather than a single reading, so daily noise doesn't throw you off.

Daily score

A 0–100 score from HRV, resting heart rate, and sleep, relative to your personal baseline. The first 14 days are a calibration period shown as provisional.

Cause tags

Tag work, sleep, exercise, caffeine and more onto your score, and look back at what may have moved it.

Timeline

See your score over day / week / month. Days with tags show as larger points.

Weekly insight

See what likely moved your score each week. These are correlations, not conclusions.

No Apple Watch? No problem

Quick 1-tap check-ins (5 levels) give you the same visualization.

Fully on-device

Everything is processed on your device. No account, no data sent off-device, no ads.

How it works

  1. Get started — connect Apple Health or choose check-in mode (you can change this later).
  2. Log — check your morning score and tag the cause with one tap. Aim for under 30 seconds a day.
  3. Reflect — on the weekend, read the weekly insight of what moved your score.

FAQ

Is an Apple Watch required?

No. Quick 1-tap check-ins give you the same visualization on their own. With a Watch, your score is computed automatically from HRV and more.

Where is my data stored?

Everything stays on your device. Nothing is sent off-device, collected, or used for advertising. No account is needed, and every feature works in airplane mode.

Why are the first 14 days provisional?

Your score is measured against your personal baseline. It grows more accurate as it learns, so the first 14 days are a calibration period.

Is it a subscription?

No. It's a one-time purchase (US$9.99). Today's score and your recent timeline are available for free.

Calmgraph is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition. Scores reflect relative trends against your personal baseline, not an absolute state of health. If you have any health concern, please consult a healthcare professional.
