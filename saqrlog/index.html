Built around weight management

Status-based targets

Each falcon keeps a flying weight and a molt weight. Saqrlog shows the latest weight, day-over-day change and 7-day average, and colors the deviation from your target.

Every record in one place

Daily weigh-ins, feeding, training (with Gulf & subcontinent terms) and hunting logs — with a per-season summary of weight and results.

Works in the field

Everything stays on your device. No account, no sync — it runs in the desert or open country with no signal.

Reports & export

Generate per-season PDF reports and export all your data as CSV whenever you want.
