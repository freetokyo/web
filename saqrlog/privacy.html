Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Saqrlog ("the app") is designed to protect your privacy by keeping everything on your device.

Information we collect

The app collects no personal information and no usage data. All records — your falcons, weights, feeding, training and hunts — are stored only on your device. Nothing is uploaded, and no account is required. The app works fully offline.

Third parties

Because no data is collected, none is shared with third parties. The app contains no analytics SDKs and no advertising SDKs.

Location

If you tap "Use current location" when adding a hunt log, the app reads your device location once to store it with that record. It stays on your device and is never uploaded. This is entirely optional.

Photos

Photos you attach to a falcon are read from your photo library with your permission and stored on your device only.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

Contact

Please reach us via the support page.
