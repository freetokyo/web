Support

Is it free?

Yes — Saqrlog is free for up to 2 falcons with all record types. Saqrlog Pro is an optional annual subscription (7-day free trial) that unlocks unlimited falcons, full weight history, per-falcon season comparison, and PDF/CSV export.

Where is my data stored?

Everything is stored on your device only. There is no account, no sync and no upload. The app works fully offline, even in airplane mode.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings inside the app and tap "Restore purchases". You can manage or cancel the subscription in the App Store: Settings → your Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Flying weight vs. molt weight?

Each falcon can have two targets. During molt, birds are intentionally kept heavier, so a single target doesn't work — set both and Saqrlog uses the right one based on the falcon's current status.

Grams or ounces?

Switch the unit anytime in Settings. Weights are stored internally and simply displayed in your chosen unit.

Which languages are supported?

Arabic, Urdu, English and Japanese, with full right-to-left layout for Arabic and Urdu.
