Home preparedness × rolling stock

Supplies and safety, all in one.

Sonalog combines rolling-stock supply management with a household emergency card. Get alerts as items approach expiry, estimate required amounts from your household size, and export your emergency card as a PDF. Everything stays on your device, and no account is required.

① 備蓄と期限アラート

水・食料・衛生・医療・電源・情報のカテゴリで在庫と賞味／消費期限を管理。期限の30日前と7日前にローカル通知でお知らせし、消費→補充のローリングストックを続けやすくします。

① Stock & expiry alerts

Manage water, food, hygiene, medical, power, and info items with quantities and expiry dates. Local notifications 30 and 7 days before expiry make it easy to keep rolling your stock.

② 必要量ガイド

家族の人数と年齢層から、水や食料のおおよその必要量を計算し、現在の在庫との差分を表示。内閣府・消防庁等の公的指針に基づく一般的な目安です。

② Required-amount guide

Estimate roughly how much water and food your household needs based on the number and age of members, and see the gap versus what you have. General guidance based on Japanese public preparedness materials.

③ 防災カルテ

非常持ち出し品のチェック、緊急連絡先・集合場所・避難所メモを記録。世帯・連絡先・集合場所・備蓄サマリを1枚のPDFにまとめ、印刷して持ち出し袋に入れられます（買い切りで解放）。

③ Emergency card

Keep a go-bag checklist, emergency contacts, meeting place, and shelter notes. Combine household, contacts, meeting place, and stock summary into one PDF to print and carry (unlocked with a one-time purchase).

ご注意

本アプリは備蓄と防災情報を記録・管理するためのものです。表示する必要量は一般的な目安であり、避難の要否や医療的な判断を行うものではありません。最終的な判断は自治体の公式情報や専門家の指針にしたがってください。

Please note

This app is for recording and managing supplies and emergency information. Required amounts are general guidance and do not determine whether to evacuate or provide medical advice. For final decisions, follow your local government's official information and professional guidance.
