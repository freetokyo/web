Support

Thank you for using Sonalog. Here are answers to common questions.

Getting started (3 easy steps)

① Add your family

In the "Requirements" tab, add family members by age group to see estimated water and food amounts for your household.

② Add your stock

In the "Stock" tab, tap + to add an item's name, category, quantity and expiry date. We notify you 30 and 7 days before expiry (when notifications are on).

③ Fill in your emergency card

In the "Emergency Card" tab, record go-bag items, contacts and your meeting place. With Full Access you can export a one-page PDF.

FAQ

Where is my data stored?

Everything is stored on your device only — no external transmission or collection. No account is required.

I'm not getting expiry notifications.

Check that notifications are allowed for the app in your device Settings, and that "Expiry notifications" is on in the app's settings. Even with notifications off, you can check expiry via the badges on each screen.

Can I keep my purchase after switching devices?

Yes — use "Restore Purchase" in the app with the same Apple ID.

How accurate are the required amounts?

They are general guidance based on Japanese public preparedness materials. The app does not determine whether to evacuate or provide medical advice. For final decisions, check your local government's official information.
