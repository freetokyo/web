Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Principles

Nailog (the "App") is designed with privacy first. It has no server and requires no account. All data you enter or save — including client information, service records, and photos — is stored only on your device.

2. Data collection

The developer does not collect, obtain, or transmit any personal information about you or your clients through the App. Its App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected".

3. Photos & camera

Service photos are taken or selected by you for your own records and stored only on your device; they are never uploaded. Camera and photo library access is used solely to attach photos to your records.

4. About iCloud

The app does not currently offer iCloud sync or backup. Your data is stored only on your device. If such a feature is offered in the future, data will be stored only in your own Apple ID's private iCloud area, and the developer will not be able to access it.

5. Third parties & analytics

The App uses no advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or tracking, and shares no data with third parties.

6. Your responsibility

Complying with the laws that apply to collecting and handling your clients' personal information is your own responsibility.
