ToScore Mini Basketball

Scoresheet & Rule Validation

Download on the App Store

ToScore is a digital scoresheet + validation app for the Table Official (TO) of minibasketball. Built to minimize recording errors, it combines active JBA rule validation with participation-time checks — all running completely offline with zero data collection.

◆ Fast Recording

  • Log points, fouls, timeouts, and player participation with minimal taps
  • Manage two teams (A/B) side by side
  • Safe undo via append-only logical deletion

◆ Automatic Validation on Every Entry

  • Instant alerts for personal fouls, accumulated T/U fouls, and team foul thresholds
  • Detects coach technicals, disqualifying fouls (D/F), and other violations
  • Possession arrow flip reminder (manual flip — no automatic reversal)

◆ Minibasket Participation-Time Check

  • Pre-game estimate of whether all players will see court time
  • Early warning when participation targets look difficult to meet

◆ Flexible Rule Profiles

  • Presets: General / U15 / Minibasket Basic / Minibasket National Special
  • Duplicate and customize any profile
  • Fine-tune periods, durations, timeouts, foul limits, and more

◆ Event History & Corrections

  • Chronological log of all events with individual undo/redo
  • Batch correction for wrong-quarter entries

◆ Export (Reference Use)

  • Share scoresheet-style image, PDF, or text
  • Output is clearly labeled "Unofficial Record / Reference Only"

◆ Team Registration

  • Pre-register team name, color, and roster for quick game setup

◆ Auto-Entry from Image (OCR)

  • Photograph or import a scoresheet or roster to auto-fill jersey numbers and names
  • All text recognition happens on-device — images are never transmitted

◆ Privacy

  • No external communication. Everything runs on-device. No data collection.

※ Records produced by this app are unofficial (reference only). Official records and competition regulations take precedence.
