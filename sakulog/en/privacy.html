Privacy Policy

SakuLog handles something deeply private: photographs of your children's work.
The Developer never receives any of it.

1. What we do not collect

The Developer collects no personal information and no usage data whatsoever. No account is required, and you are never asked for a name, email address or phone number. There are no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs and no tracking tools in the App.

2. Where your data lives

Artwork photos, your child's name and birth month, notes, storage statuses and storage rules are stored only on your device and in your own iCloud (private database).

  • This data is never sent to a developer server (the Developer does not operate one).
  • The Developer cannot view, retrieve or store any of it.
  • Data held in iCloud is handled under Apple's privacy policy and the iCloud terms of service.
  • It syncs only between your own devices signed in to the same Apple Account. It is never shared with other Apple Accounts.

3. Camera

The camera is used to photograph artwork. Images are processed on device and saved only to your device and your own iCloud. They are never transmitted elsewhere. You can revoke camera access at any time in iOS Settings.

4. Photo library

"Choose from Photos" uses the standard iOS photo picker. With that mechanism only the photos you select are handed to the App; the App never gains access to your whole photo library.

5. On-device processing

Perspective correction, trimming and summary generation all happen entirely on your device. No images or text are transmitted for processing.

6. In-app purchase

"Full access" is purchased through Apple's App Store. The Developer never receives payment details such as card numbers.

7. Children's privacy

The App is intended for parents and is not designed for use by children themselves (it is not offered in the Kids category). It does handle data about children, but all of it is stored solely on the parent's device and in the parent's own iCloud, and is never transmitted to the Developer or any third party.

8. Third parties

Because the Developer obtains no data, none is provided to third parties.

9. Deleting your data

Artworks and children's records can be removed inside the App. Deleting the App erases the data held on the device. Data in iCloud can be managed and deleted from iOS Settings > Apple Account > iCloud.

10. Changes

Any revision to this policy will be announced on this page.
