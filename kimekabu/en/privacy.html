Privacy Policy

Privacy policy for Kimekabu ("the App").

Data collection

The App does not collect or transmit any personal information. All data — decision records, executions, analysis — is stored only on your device and is never sent to the developer or any third party.

Network

The App works fully offline. It does not link to brokerage accounts or communicate with external servers.

Account

No account registration is required.

In-app purchase

The one-time in-app purchase uses Apple's standard StoreKit. Purchase data is processed by Apple; the developer does not retain payment information.

Contact

For questions about this policy, please contact us via the Support page.
