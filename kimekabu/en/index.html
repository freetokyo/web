Kimekabu

Every move, with a reason.

Record why you buy in about 90 seconds, then reconcile against your plan when you sell. A trade journal that turns your decision patterns into numbers.

Download on the App Store

iPhone · iOS 26+ · Free (in-app purchase)

Features

📝

Decision cards

Structure ticker, hypothesis, reason tags, conviction and target/stop in ~90 seconds before you enter.

⚖️

Reconcile on exit

At exit, auto-compare against your plan (target, stop, horizon) and record deviation reasons and lessons.

📊

Decision analysis

Win rate by reason tag, conviction vs. outcome, rule-deviation rate and holding-period distribution.

🔒

Fully offline

No brokerage linking. All data stays on your device. No account, works fully in airplane mode.

💴

One-time purchase

No subscription. Free up to 10 cards; Pro (one-time) unlocks unlimited cards, full analysis and export.

📤

Export

Export your decision records as CSV / PDF for backup and review.

This app is a tool to record and organize your own trading decisions. It does not recommend or solicit any financial product, nor advise on buy/sell timing. All figures shown are summaries of your own past records and do not guarantee future results. Make your own investment decisions at your own responsibility.
