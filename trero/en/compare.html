Who Should Choose Trero?

Each app has a different sweet spot. Here is what makes Trero the right choice — and when you might prefer something else.

Choose Trero if you...

  • Want to track workout and body composition in one app
  • Log sets from Apple Watch without a subscription
  • Want per-exercise goals with stall-detection and next-set suggestions
  • Use Live Activity or widgets to track rest timers from your lock screen
  • Prefer a clean, focused iPhone-first experience

Choose Strong if you...

  • Use both iPhone and Android and want the same app on both
  • Already have a Strong subscription and have years of history there
  • Want a large community and extensive exercise library

Choose Hevy if you...

  • Want cross-platform (iOS + Android) workout sharing with friends
  • Prefer a social feed at the center of your logging workflow
  • Do not need body composition tracking inside the same app

Common Questions

Is Trero free compared to Strong and Hevy? All three apps offer a free plan. Trero's free plan includes core logging, Apple Watch support, goal tracking, and body metrics with no time limit. Pro unlocks unlimited history, CSV export, the plate calculator, and 1RM rank. No subscription is required to start logging.
Does Trero support Apple Watch like Strong? Yes. Trero supports logging sets directly from Apple Watch, with previous weight and reps displayed on the Watch screen. Sets sync to iPhone history automatically. Unlike Strong, Apple Watch support in Trero is available on the free plan.
Which app is best for tracking body composition? Trero is the only app in this comparison with built-in body composition tracking (weight, body fat, muscle rate, waist, BMI, lean mass) and Apple Health write/import — all in the same app as workout logging.
Can I switch from Strong or Hevy to Trero? Trero does not currently import from other apps directly. You can start fresh with a free account, or contact support if you have a specific data migration question.
