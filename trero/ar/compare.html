Who Trero fits best

Trero is a strong fit for some use cases and an honest miss for others.

✅ Great fit if you...

  • Want workout log and body metrics in one place
  • Use Apple Watch and want to log from your wrist for free
  • Want goal-based training with next-set suggestions
  • Want Live Activity and widgets for rest timing
  • Prefer a clean, iPhone-first experience

⚠️ Less ideal if you...

  • Need Android support — Trero is iPhone only
  • Have years of data in another app with no import path
  • Want AI-generated programs rather than self-directed logging
  • Need a very large built-in exercise library with video demos

Common questions

Is Trero a good alternative to Strong?

Trero is worth trying if you want built-in body composition tracking, per-exercise goal setting, Live Activity rest timer, and Apple Watch logging — all available without a paid subscription. Trero is iPhone only; Strong supports Android too.

Is Trero a good alternative to Hevy?

Trero is worth trying if you want Apple Watch support, body metrics alongside your workout history, and per-exercise goals in a single iPhone-focused app. Hevy supports Android; Trero does not.

Can I import data from Strong or Hevy?

Trero does not currently have a direct import feature from other apps. You would start fresh. Contact support if you have a specific data migration question.

Does Apple Watch logging require Pro?

No. Apple Watch set logging is included in the free plan. Log sets from your wrist and sync to iPhone history without a subscription.

Is Trero free to download?

Yes. Core logging, Apple Watch support, goal tracking, and body metrics are all available on the free plan. No credit card or trial — just download and start.
