Your records are your coaching edge.

A client chart for personal trainers. Session logs and prepaid packages — all on your device.

Download on the App Store

Last session, right there

The Today screen lists your sessions for the day, plus recent clients with what they did last time. Start recording and their previous weights and reps are already filled in — leave them as-is if nothing changed.

Passes count themselves down

Saving a session uses one session from the linked pass. Low balance (2 or fewer) and near expiry (within 14 days) are shown by both colour and wording, so nothing gets missed or billed twice.

See the change as a chart

Record weight, body fat, and girth (chest, waist, hip, thigh, arm) and see the trend and net change over any period — something concrete to show your client.

Notes you need on the floor

Keep each client's goal and things to watch ("right shoulder discomfort; go easy on overhead work") on their chart. They show up on the Today screen too.

Presets and templates

17 exercises grouped by body part — bench press, squat, deadlift and more — come built in. Save the sets you use often as a template and add them all at once (3 on the free tier, unlimited with Pro).

Your clients' data stays put

ToreKarte has no server and no account. What you enter is stored only on your device and is never sent to the developer or any third party.

Lock and backup are free

App lock (Face ID, Touch ID, or passcode) and backup export/restore are included in the free version. Safety and data-protection features shouldn't sit behind a paywall.

Session reminders

Set the next session when you record, and this device reminds you an hour before.

Pricing

  • Free: up to 3 clients and 3 templates. Session records, charts, passes, app lock, reminders, and backup are all included.
  • ToreKarte Pro (one-time, ¥3,000): unlimited clients and templates, plus a PDF progress report to hand to clients, CSV export, and a monthly summary (sessions delivered and estimated revenue). Not a subscription. Buy once, keep it forever.

iPhone only (iOS 18 or later). Available in Japanese and English. ToreKarte is a record-keeping tool; it does not provide medical advice or diagnosis. Passes record sessions left, expiry and unpaid status only — no payments are handled.
