Netlit

Dose from the volume your tank actually holds
Life

A 60 cm tank measured from the outside comes to 64.8 L. Take away the glass, the water line, the substrate and the hardscape and about 48 L is left — and that is the number every dose should be based on. Netlit fixes that figure once, then runs water changes, dry salt dosing and dechlorinator on top of it, and drops the result straight into your log. Secondary ions such as the potassium in KNO3 are always shown. Targets a water change cannot reach return advice, not a number. Fully offline, no account, no data collection.

What is freetokyo apps?

A collection of iOS apps built by indie developer freetokyo. Across four areas — health, life, money and work — it offers 60 small tools that make daily chores a little lighter.

Is it free to use?

All core features are free. To support ongoing development, operation, support and improvement, some apps offer premium features via one-time purchase, or occasionally subscription, at around the price of a cup of coffee. They are designed so you get a fully sufficient experience for everyday use without going premium.

How is my personal information and data handled?

Many apps are fully offline, or use only your own cloud such as iCloud, and do not send data to the developer’s servers. See each app’s privacy policy for details.
