Our Philosophy

Small tools, a lighter everyday

Across the three areas of health, life and work, we build tools that work in your own hands — without relying on charges or data collection.

💚
Health: don’t get ruled by data

Scores are a guide. Designed as a reference line for choosing sustainable habits alongside how you actually feel.

🧡
Life: share the load as a family

So the burden doesn’t fall on one person, the flow of sharing, recording and checking is kept simple enough for anyone to follow.

💙
Work: never get in the way of the day job

Light enough for someone on-site to finish a task in a one-minute break. Works offline and replaces paper and stamps.

What is freetokyo apps?

A collection of iOS apps built by indie developer freetokyo. Across three areas — health, life and work — it offers small tools that make daily chores a little lighter.

Is it free to use?

All core features are free. To support ongoing development, operation, support and improvement, some apps offer premium features via subscription or one-time purchase, at around the price of a cup of coffee. They are designed so you get a fully sufficient experience for everyday use without going premium.

How is my personal information and data handled?

Many apps are fully offline, or use only your own cloud such as iCloud. See each app’s privacy policy for details.
