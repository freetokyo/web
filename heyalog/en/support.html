Support

ヘヤログ

How do I get started?
From the 'Home' screen, tap '+' in the top right corner to register a property. Enter the property name and type (apartment, condominium, etc.) and add rooms (living room, bathroom, etc.). Then simply take photos for each checkpoint in each room.
How do I use move-in and move-out records separately?
You can switch between 'Move-in' and 'Move-out' using the segment on the property details screen. Photos taken at move-in and move-out photos from the same angle can be compared side by side, making it easy to verify which damages existed before move-in.
Can checklist items be customized?
Checkpoints are set automatically according to ministry guidelines based on the room type (kitchen, bathroom, room, etc.). In the current version, items cannot be added or deleted.
Can GPS be recorded in photos?
If you turn on 'Settings → Add location to photos', the latitude and longitude of the current location will be recorded in photos when they are taken. The default is off. Location information is stored only on the device and is not sent externally.
Can I import existing photos (from the camera roll)?
On the checkpoint screen, tap the camera button and select 'Select from Library' to use already-taken photos.
Are there restrictions on photo quality or number?
Multiple photos can be taken per checkpoint (no limit). Photos are saved in full size, so more properties and photos consume more device storage. Deleting unnecessary records is recommended.
How do I create a PDF?
Tap 'Create PDF Report' on the property details screen. You can select the language (Japanese or English) and generate it; after generation, you can send it by email, save it to the Files app, or print it via the share button.
What does the PDF contain?
It includes: property name, room name, photos of each checkpoint with recording date and time, location information (if recorded), and inspection status. At move-out, move-in and move-out photos are displayed comparatively side by side.
What can I use for free?
Photo recording at move-in, the checklist, and creating PDF reports are completely free. Same-angle comparison photography at move-out, the comparative PDF report, and bulk data export (ZIP) are unlocked with a one-time purchase.
Is there a subscription?
No. It is a one-time purchase for ¥600. Once purchased, it can be used permanently without additional costs.
I want to restore my purchase
Tap 'Settings → Restore Purchases'. If you are logged in with the same Apple ID, you can restore for free even after changing devices.
Where is data stored?
All data (property information, photos, inspection records) is stored only on the user's device. Nothing is sent to or collected on external servers.
Is data deleted when the app is deleted?
Yes, when the app is deleted, all data on the device is also deleted. It is recommended to save important photos elsewhere in advance using 'Export Data (ZIP)' (one-time purchase feature).
Can I transfer data when changing devices?
Using iOS 'iCloud Backup' or 'Direct Transfer between iPhones', app data can also be transferred. This app does not have its own cloud synchronization function.
