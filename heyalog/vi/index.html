Heyalog - Room Condition Log

Protect your deposit with reco

Tải xuống trên App Store

Heyalog helps tenants document room conditions with photos at move-in and move-out, following Japan's Ministry of Land guidelines. Generate PDF reports to protect your security deposit.

Core Features (Free)

  • Location-based photo checklists (entrance, bedroom, kitchen, bathroom, toilet, balcony)
  • MLIT-compliant checklist: scratches, stains, damage, equipment issues
  • PDF report: photo registry, checklist, disclaimer, SHA-256 hash appendix

Premium Features (One-Time ¥600)

  • Move-out same-angle comparison: overlay move-in photos with transparency
  • Comparison PDF for landlords
  • Data export ZIP

Privacy

  • Fully offline - no network
  • Data stored only on iPhone
  • No ads, no tracking, one-time purchase
