Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Unelog ("the app") puts your privacy first.

Information we collect

The app collects no personal information or usage data whatsoever. Records such as plots, ridges, plantings, visits, and contracts are all stored on your device and are never sent externally. No account registration is required.

Photo access

When you attach a photo to a planting record, only the image you select through the iOS photo picker is imported into the app. The app does not access your entire photo library and does not send anything externally.

Notifications

Contract reminders are local notifications that stay entirely on your device. Notification content is never sent to an external server.

Weather feature and location

Only when you turn on the weather feature (full access, off by default) are your plot's coordinates sent to Apple's weather service (WeatherKit) to fetch weather information. The coordinates are used only to fetch weather; the app does not store, collect, or share them with third parties. Your cultivation records are never sent. You can stop using location at any time from iOS Settings.

Third-party sharing

Because no data is collected, there is nothing to share with third parties. We use no analytics SDKs and no advertising SDKs.

Contact

Please reach us via the support page.
