Frequently asked questions

Is it free to use?
Yes. One plot with up to 6 ridges and the core records (plantings, visits, photos, rotation warnings, and the planting calendar) are free to use. Removing those limits, rotation suggestions, unlimited contract reminders, the profitability dashboard, weather, PDF export, and the season summary are unlocked with full access — a one-time purchase (no subscription).
Does the weather feature send my location?
Only if you turn on the weather feature (off by default) are your plot's coordinates sent to Apple's weather service to fetch conditions. Your records are never sent, and the app does not store or collect your coordinates.
I want to restore my purchase
Go to Settings → "Restore Purchase". If you have already purchased with the same Apple ID, it restores for free.
Where is my data stored?
Everything is stored on your device and nothing is sent externally. No account is required. Backups are left to your device (the OS device backup, including iCloud).
Are the crop-rotation intervals accurate?
The interval years shown are general guidelines and vary with variety and soil conditions. Please use them as reference information.
I'm not receiving reminder notifications
In iOS, check that notifications are allowed under "Settings → Notifications → Unelog". Also confirm the reminder date is not in the past.
Where can I add photos?
From the planting record screen, you can select and attach images from your device's photo library.
