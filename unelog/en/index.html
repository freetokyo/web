Unelog

A fully offline cultivation log built for the rented plot at a community or allotment garden — everything from contract to harvest in one place.

Download on the App Store

Plots and ridges

Manage your plot and the ridges within it as a simple bar list. See at a glance which ridges are growing and which are empty.

Planting records

For each ridge, record the crop, sowing / transplanting / harvest dates, a photo, and notes. Pick a crop from the built-in plant dictionary or enter your own.

Visit log

Log a visit with one tap. The home screen always shows days since your last visit, so you know the interval even for a plot you travel to.

Contract & reminders

Record your contract period and rent, and get a local notification on your device when renewal or lottery-application deadlines approach.

Rotation check

Unelog maps each crop to its family and warns you, using general guideline intervals, when the same family is planted again too soon on the same ridge. Full access also suggests rotations.

Profitability dashboard (full access)

Estimate your net from harvest values, materials, and rent. See at a glance whether the plot is paying for itself.

Planting calendar

See typical sowing and harvest months for common crops at a glance.

Weather & frost (full access, opt-in)

Apple Weather shows conditions and frost risk at your plot's location.

Season PDF & summary (full access)

Export a year of records as a PDF and generate a season summary.

Free to start / full access is a one-time purchase (not a subscription).

No account required. Your records stay on your device — nothing is sent externally and no data is collected. Only if you turn on the weather feature are your plot's coordinates sent to Apple's weather service (your records are never sent).

Rotation intervals are general guidelines and vary by variety and soil. They do not guarantee cultivation results.
